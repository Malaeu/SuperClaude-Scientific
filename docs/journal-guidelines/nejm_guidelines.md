# New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM) - Author Guidelines

**Source**: https://www.nejm.org/author-center/submission-guidelines  
**Updated**: August 2025  
**Journal Impact Factor**: 158.5 (2024)

## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

### Original Articles
- **Abstract**: 250 words maximum
- **Main Text**: 3,500 words maximum (excluding abstract, references, figures, tables)
- **Methods**: Can be moved to supplementary material if over word limit
- **References**: 50 maximum
- **Figures**: 4 maximum
- **Tables**: 3 maximum

### Brief Reports  
- **Abstract**: 150 words maximum
- **Main Text**: 1,200 words maximum
- **References**: 15 maximum
- **Figures**: 2 maximum
- **Tables**: 1 maximum

### Special Articles
- **Abstract**: 250 words maximum
- **Main Text**: 4,000 words maximum
- **References**: 75 maximum

## Reference Style Requirements

### Format: Vancouver Style
- **Style**: Numbered references in superscript
- **Order**: Sequential order of first citation in text
- **Format Example**: 
  ```
  1. Smith J, Jones A, Brown C. Title of article. N Engl J Med. 2024;390:123-130.
  ```

### Reference Details Required
- All authors (up to 6); if more than 6, list first 6 followed by "et al."
- Complete article title
- Journal abbreviation (as in Index Medicus)
- Year, volume, inclusive page numbers
- DOI when available

## Figure Requirements

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI minimum at final print size
- **Width**: Single column (86 mm) or double column (174 mm)
- **File Formats**: TIFF, EPS, or high-resolution PDF preferred
- **Color**: RGB for online; CMYK for print if color essential

### Figure Content Guidelines
- **Panels**: Multiple panels labeled A, B, C, etc.
- **Legends**: Comprehensive, explaining all symbols and abbreviations
- **Statistics**: Include sample sizes, p-values, confidence intervals
- **Scale Bars**: Required for all microscopic images
- **Patient Images**: Written consent required; faces must be masked

### Figure Types
- **Clinical Images**: High resolution, professional quality
- **Graphs/Charts**: Clear axis labels, appropriate statistical representation
- **Kaplan-Meier Curves**: Include risk tables, confidence intervals
- **Forest Plots**: Standard formatting with study weights

## Table Requirements

### Format Standards
- **Structure**: Simple grid format, no vertical lines
- **Headers**: Clear column and row headers
- **Footnotes**: Superscript letters (a, b, c) for explanatory notes
- **Statistics**: Include measures of variability (SD, SEM, CI)

### Content Requirements
- **Baseline Characteristics**: Demographics, clinical variables
- **Outcomes**: Primary and secondary endpoints clearly identified
- **Statistical Tests**: Appropriate tests with p-values
- **Sample Sizes**: N values for all groups/subgroups

## Statistical Requirements

### Statistical Methods
- **Analysis Plan**: Pre-specified primary and secondary analyses
- **Software**: Specify statistical software and version
- **Significance Level**: α = 0.05 unless otherwise specified
- **Multiple Comparisons**: Appropriate corrections when applicable

### Required Statistical Information
- **Sample Size Calculation**: Power analysis for primary endpoint
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Mean ± SD, median (IQR), n (%)
- **Inferential Statistics**: Test statistic, degrees of freedom, exact p-values
- **Confidence Intervals**: 95% CI for effect estimates
- **Missing Data**: Handling and sensitivity analyses

## Ethical Requirements

### Human Studies
- **IRB Approval**: Institutional review board approval required
- **Informed Consent**: Documentation of participant consent
- **Clinical Trials**: Registration in clinical trials registry
- **Data Sharing**: Statement on data availability

### Animal Studies
- **IACUC Approval**: Institutional animal care committee approval
- **Guidelines Compliance**: ARRIVE guidelines for animal research
- **Welfare Standards**: Humane care and use standards

## Supplementary Material

### Allowable Content
- **Extended Methods**: Detailed methodology exceeding word limits  
- **Additional Figures**: Supporting data not essential for main story
- **Additional Tables**: Comprehensive data sets
- **Statistical Code**: Analysis scripts and additional analyses
- **Video Content**: Surgical procedures, imaging sequences

### Format Requirements
- **File Formats**: PDF for text, standard formats for multimedia
- **Size Limits**: 10 MB per file, 50 MB total per submission
- **Labeling**: Clear file naming convention

## Submission Requirements

### Cover Letter
- **Significance Statement**: Why suitable for NEJM readership
- **Ethics Statement**: Confirmation of ethical approval
- **Conflict of Interest**: Financial and personal conflicts
- **Author Contributions**: Specific contributions of each author

### Required Documents
- **Manuscript File**: Word document, double-spaced
- **Figure Files**: High-resolution separate files
- **ICMJE Forms**: Conflict of interest forms for all authors
- **Ethics Documentation**: IRB approval letters
- **Clinical Trial Documentation**: Registration certificate

## Review Process

### Timeline
- **Initial Review**: 2-3 weeks for editorial decision
- **Peer Review**: 4-6 weeks if sent for external review
- **Revision Period**: 6 weeks for revised submission

### Acceptance Criteria
- **Novelty**: Significant advance in medical knowledge
- **Clinical Impact**: Relevance to clinical practice
- **Methodological Rigor**: Sound study design and analysis
- **Clarity**: Clear presentation accessible to general medical audience

## Special Considerations

### COVID-19 Research
- **Fast Track**: Expedited review for urgent public health relevance
- **Preprint Policy**: Allowed on preprint servers during review

### Clinical Trials
- **Registration**: Must be registered before enrollment
- **CONSORT Compliance**: Follow CONSORT reporting guidelines
- **Data Monitoring**: Independent data monitoring committee for large trials

### Systematic Reviews/Meta-analyses
- **PRISMA Compliance**: Follow PRISMA reporting guidelines
- **Protocol Registration**: PROSPERO registration preferred
- **Search Strategy**: Comprehensive search of multiple databases
