# The Lancet - Author Guidelines

**Source**: https://www.thelancet.com/lancet/information-for-authors  
**Updated**: August 2025  
**Journal Impact Factor**: 202.7 (2024)

## Article Types and Word Limits

### Articles (Original Research)
- **Summary**: 300 words maximum (structured: Background, Methods, Findings, Interpretation)
- **Main Text**: 4,500 words maximum (excluding summary, panel, references, figures, tables)
- **References**: 40 maximum
- **Figures**: 5 maximum  
- **Tables**: 5 maximum
- **Panel**: 1 research in context panel (150 words)

### Correspondence
- **Text**: 400 words maximum
- **References**: 5 maximum
- **Figures**: 1 maximum
- **Authors**: 5 maximum

### Comments
- **Text**: 1,200 words maximum
- **References**: 15 maximum
- **Figures**: 2 maximum

### Reviews (Seminar/Personal View)
- **Summary**: 150 words maximum (unstructured)
- **Main Text**: 3,500 words maximum
- **References**: 60 maximum
- **Figures**: 4 maximum

## Reference Style: Vancouver System

### Format Requirements
- **Style**: Numbered references in square brackets [1]
- **Order**: Sequential order of first mention in text
- **Multiple References**: [1,3,5] or [1-3] for consecutive numbers

### Reference Format Examples
```
Journal Article:
1. Smith J, Jones A, Brown C. Treatment outcomes in liver transplantation. Lancet 2024; 403: 123-30.

Book:
2. Wilson D, Taylor M. Clinical hepatology, 3rd ed. London: Academic Press, 2024.

Book Chapter:
3. Davis R. Immunosuppression protocols. In: Lee K, ed. Transplant medicine. New York: Medical Publishers, 2024: 45-67.
```

### Electronic References
- **DOI**: Include when available
- **URLs**: Include access date for web content
- **Format**: Same as print but add DOI or URL

## Figure Specifications

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI minimum for photographs, 600 DPI for line art
- **Size**: Maximum width 174 mm (double column) or 84 mm (single column)
- **File Format**: TIFF, EPS, or PDF (vector graphics preferred)
- **Color Space**: RGB for web, CMYK for print

### Figure Content Standards
- **Legends**: Comprehensive descriptions below figures
- **Labels**: Clear, legible text and symbols
- **Statistical Information**: Sample sizes, p-values in legends
- **Multiple Panels**: Labeled A, B, C, etc. in upper left corners
- **Scale Bars**: Required for all microscopy images

### Special Figure Types
- **Kaplan-Meier Plots**: Include numbers at risk, confidence intervals
- **Forest Plots**: Follow standard meta-analysis formatting
- **Clinical Photos**: Written patient consent required; anonymization essential
- **Radiological Images**: High contrast, appropriate windowing

## Table Guidelines

### Structure Requirements
- **Format**: Simple structure with minimal formatting
- **Headers**: Clear column headers with units
- **Footnotes**: Use superscript symbols (*, †, ‡, §)
- **Alignment**: Numerical data right-aligned, text left-aligned

### Content Standards
- **Statistical Presentation**: Appropriate measures of central tendency and dispersion
- **P-values**: Exact values when p > 0.001; otherwise p < 0.001
- **Confidence Intervals**: 95% CI for effect estimates
- **Sample Sizes**: Clear indication of denominators

### Common Table Types
- **Table 1**: Baseline characteristics (demographics, clinical variables)
- **Table 2**: Primary and secondary outcomes
- **Table 3**: Adverse events or subgroup analyses

## Summary (Abstract) Structure

### Structured Summary Format
#### Background
- **Content**: Rationale for study, knowledge gaps
- **Length**: 2-3 sentences maximum

#### Methods
- **Study Design**: Clear description of methodology
- **Participants**: Population, setting, inclusion/exclusion criteria
- **Intervention**: Treatment details (for trials)
- **Outcomes**: Primary and key secondary endpoints
- **Analysis**: Statistical approach

#### Findings
- **Dates**: Study recruitment period
- **Participants**: Numbers enrolled, analyzed
- **Results**: Primary outcome with statistical measures
- **Key Secondary Outcomes**: Important secondary findings

#### Interpretation
- **Clinical Significance**: Clinical implications of findings
- **Limitations**: Major study limitations
- **Generalizability**: Broader applicability

## Research in Context Panel

### Required Content (150 words)
#### Evidence before this study
- **Search Strategy**: Brief description of literature search
- **Key Findings**: What was previously known
- **Knowledge Gaps**: What remained unknown

#### Added value of this study
- **Novel Contributions**: What this study adds to existing knowledge
- **Methodological Advances**: Improvements in approach or methods

#### Implications of all the available evidence
- **Clinical Practice**: How findings might change practice
- **Future Research**: What questions remain

## Statistical Requirements

### Study Design and Analysis
- **Primary Endpoint**: Clearly defined single primary outcome
- **Sample Size**: Power calculation with assumptions
- **Randomization**: Method and stratification factors (for trials)
- **Blinding**: Description of masking procedures
- **Analysis Plan**: Pre-specified statistical analysis plan

### Statistical Reporting Standards
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Appropriate summary measures
- **Effect Measures**: Relative risks, odds ratios, hazard ratios with 95% CI
- **P-values**: Two-sided tests unless justified otherwise
- **Multiple Comparisons**: Adjustment for multiple testing when appropriate
- **Missing Data**: Handling and sensitivity analyses

### Required Statistical Information
- **Software**: Statistical software and version
- **Tests Used**: Specific statistical tests applied
- **Model Assumptions**: Verification of statistical assumptions
- **Sensitivity Analyses**: Robustness of findings

## Ethical Standards

### Human Research Ethics
- **Ethics Approval**: Research ethics committee/IRB approval
- **Informed Consent**: Written informed consent from participants
- **Vulnerable Populations**: Special protections and considerations
- **Data Protection**: Compliance with data protection regulations (GDPR)

### Clinical Trial Requirements
- **Registration**: Prospective registration in WHO Primary Registry
- **Protocol**: Full protocol available on request
- **CONSORT**: Adherence to CONSORT reporting guidelines
- **Data Monitoring**: Independent data monitoring committee for phase III trials

### Animal Research
- **Ethics Committee**: Animal ethics committee approval
- **Guidelines**: Compliance with international guidelines (e.g., NC3Rs)
- **Welfare**: Humane endpoints and welfare monitoring

## Conflict of Interest and Funding

### Required Disclosures
- **Financial Interests**: All financial relationships with commercial entities
- **Institutional Affiliations**: Employment and consulting relationships
- **Research Funding**: All sources of research funding
- **Intellectual Property**: Patents and patent applications

### ICMJE Forms
- **All Authors**: Completed ICMJE conflict of interest forms required
- **Updates**: Annual updates for ongoing relationships

## Submission Process

### Initial Submission Requirements
- **Cover Letter**: Significance and suitability for Lancet audience
- **Manuscript**: Word document, double-spaced, line numbers
- **Figures**: High-resolution separate files
- **Tables**: Embedded in manuscript or separate files
- **Supplementary Material**: Clearly labeled additional files

### Peer Review Process
- **Editorial Assessment**: Initial editorial screening (1-2 weeks)
- **External Review**: Expert peer review if editorially suitable (3-4 weeks)  
- **Decision Timeline**: Target 6-8 weeks from submission
- **Revision**: 4 weeks allowed for revision

## Supplementary Material Guidelines

### Acceptable Content
- **Extended Methods**: Detailed methodology
- **Additional Analyses**: Subgroup analyses, sensitivity analyses
- **Data Tables**: Complete datasets supporting main findings
- **Protocols**: Study protocols and statistical analysis plans
- **Media Files**: Video content, audio files

### Format and Size Limits
- **File Size**: 10 MB per file maximum
- **Total Size**: 100 MB maximum per submission
- **Formats**: PDF for text, standard formats for media

## Language and Style

### Writing Standards
- **Language**: Clear, concise English
- **Tense**: Past tense for methods and results
- **Person**: Third person preferred
- **Abbreviations**: Defined at first use, limited use encouraged

### Lancet Style Specifics
- **Numbers**: Spell out one through nine, use numerals for 10 and above
- **Units**: International System of Units (SI units)
- **Dates**: Day month year format (e.g., 15 July 2024)
- **Gene Names**: Follow HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee guidelines

## Special Requirements

### Global Health Research
- **GATHER**: Adherence to GATHER guidelines for health estimates
- **Local Collaboration**: Evidence of meaningful local partnership
- **Capacity Building**: Contribution to local research capacity

### Infectious Disease Research
- **Strain Information**: Complete pathogen identification
- **Sequence Data**: Deposition in public databases when applicable
- **Surveillance Data**: Use of established surveillance definitions
