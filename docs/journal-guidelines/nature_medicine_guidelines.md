# Nature Medicine - Author Guidelines

**Source**: https://www.nature.com/nm/for-authors  
**Updated**: August 2025  
**Journal Impact Factor**: 82.9 (2024)

## Article Types and Length Limits

### Articles (Original Research)
- **Abstract**: 150 words maximum (unstructured)
- **Main Text**: 3,000 words maximum (excluding abstract, methods, references, figure legends)
- **Methods**: No word limit (can be in supplementary information)
- **References**: No strict limit (typically 50-60)
- **Figures**: 4 maximum in main text
- **Display Items**: Total of 6 maximum (figures + tables)

### Letters (Brief Communications)
- **Abstract**: Not required
- **Main Text**: 1,000 words maximum
- **Methods**: Brief, essential details only
- **References**: 15 maximum
- **Figures**: 2 maximum

### Reviews
- **Abstract**: 150 words maximum
- **Main Text**: 4,000 words maximum
- **References**: No strict limit
- **Figures**: 5 maximum

### Perspectives/Comments
- **Abstract**: Not required
- **Main Text**: 1,500 words maximum
- **References**: 30 maximum
- **Figures**: 2 maximum

## Reference Format: Nature Style

### Basic Format
- **Style**: Numbered references (no brackets, no superscript)
- **Order**: Sequential order of first citation
- **Format**: Author, Title. Journal vol, pages (year)

### Reference Examples
```
Journal Article:
1. Smith, J. & Jones, A. Hepatocyte regeneration in liver disease. Nat. Med. 30, 123–135 (2024).

Multi-author (>2):
2. Brown, C. et al. Mechanisms of immunosuppression. Cell 189, 456–467 (2024).

Book:
3. Wilson, D. & Taylor, M. Clinical Hepatology 3rd edn (Academic Press, 2024).

Book Chapter:
4. Davis, R. in Transplant Medicine (ed. Lee, K.) 45–67 (Medical Publishers, 2024).
```

### Electronic References
- **DOI**: Include when available
- **Preprints**: Include server name and article identifier
- **URLs**: For databases, software, with access date

## Figure Requirements

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution**: 300 dpi for color/grayscale, 600 dpi for line art
- **Size**: Maximum width 183 mm (full page width)
- **File Format**: TIFF, EPS, PDF (vector preferred for graphs)
- **Color**: RGB color space for digital display

### Figure Composition
- **Multi-panel Figures**: Use lowercase letters (a, b, c) for panels
- **Size Consistency**: Consistent font sizes across all panels (typically 7-9 pt)
- **Labels**: Clear, readable labels and symbols
- **Scale Bars**: Required for microscopy images with size indicated

### Statistical Requirements in Figures
- **Error Bars**: Must specify what they represent (s.e.m., s.d., 95% CI)
- **Sample Sizes**: Include n values in figure legends
- **Statistical Tests**: Specify statistical tests used
- **P-values**: Use appropriate significance indicators (*, **, ***)

### Specialized Figure Types
- **Western Blots**: Show molecular weight markers, loading controls
- **Microscopy**: High resolution, appropriate magnification
- **Flow Cytometry**: Include gating strategies, controls
- **Clinical Images**: Patient consent required, anonymization essential

## Methods Section Requirements

### Study Design
- **Study Type**: Clear description (prospective, retrospective, cross-sectional, etc.)
- **Setting**: Geographic location, institutional setting
- **Time Period**: Study duration and follow-up periods
- **Approvals**: Ethics committee approvals and registration numbers

### Participants/Subjects
- **Humans**: Inclusion/exclusion criteria, demographics
- **Animals**: Species, strain, age, sex, housing conditions
- **Cell Lines**: Source, authentication, mycoplasma testing
- **Sample Size**: Power calculations and statistical justification

### Procedures and Interventions
- **Detailed Protocols**: Sufficient detail for replication
- **Reagents**: Commercial sources with catalog numbers
- **Equipment**: Manufacturer, model numbers
- **Software**: Name, version, company

### Statistical Analysis
- **Software**: Statistical software and version
- **Tests**: Specific statistical tests with justification
- **Multiple Comparisons**: Corrections applied
- **Significance**: Definition of statistical significance (typically P < 0.05)

## Reporting Guidelines Compliance

### Clinical Studies
- **CONSORT**: Randomized controlled trials
- **STROBE**: Observational studies
- **STARD**: Diagnostic accuracy studies
- **PRISMA**: Systematic reviews and meta-analyses

### Preclinical Studies
- **ARRIVE**: Animal research
- **MIQE**: qPCR experiments
- **MDAR**: Materials Design Analysis Reporting

### Study Registration
- **Clinical Trials**: ClinicalTrials.gov or WHO Primary Registry
- **Animal Studies**: Not required but recommended
- **Systematic Reviews**: PROSPERO registration preferred

## Data Availability and Sharing

### Data Availability Statement
- **Required**: All articles must include data availability statement
- **Location**: At end of Methods section
- **Scope**: Raw data, processed data, code availability

### Data Deposition Requirements
- **Genomic Data**: GEO, SRA, or European equivalent
- **Structural Data**: Protein Data Bank (PDB)
- **Clinical Data**: Restricted access repositories when appropriate
- **Software/Code**: GitHub, Zenodo, or institutional repositories

### Data Sharing Considerations
- **Patient Privacy**: De-identification requirements
- **Ethical Constraints**: IRB-approved sharing restrictions
- **Commercial Restrictions**: Industry collaboration limitations

## Ethical Requirements

### Human Research
- **IRB Approval**: Institutional review board approval required
- **Informed Consent**: Written consent from all participants
- **Vulnerable Populations**: Special protections for children, pregnant women
- **International Studies**: Local ethics approvals required

### Animal Research
- **IACUC Approval**: Institutional animal care committee approval
- **Guidelines**: Compliance with national and international guidelines
- **Humane Endpoints**: Clear termination criteria
- **Housing**: Standard laboratory conditions

### Research Integrity
- **Misconduct**: Zero tolerance for fabrication, falsification, plagiarism
- **Image Manipulation**: Guidelines for appropriate image processing
- **Authorship**: ICMJE authorship criteria
- **Conflict of Interest**: Full disclosure required

## Conflict of Interest and Funding

### Financial Disclosures
- **Industry Funding**: All commercial funding sources
- **Consulting**: Advisory board participation
- **Equity**: Stock ownership in relevant companies  
- **Patents**: Intellectual property related to research

### Author Contributions
- **CRediT System**: Use standardized contribution roles
- **Specific Contributions**: Detailed description of each author's role
- **Corresponding Author**: Responsible for integrity of entire work

## Supplementary Information

### Content Guidelines
- **Extended Methods**: Detailed protocols exceeding main text limits
- **Additional Figures**: Supporting data not essential for main story
- **Tables**: Large datasets, comprehensive statistical analyses
- **Videos**: Surgical procedures, cell behavior, imaging sequences
- **Code**: Analysis scripts, computational methods

### Format Requirements
- **File Naming**: Descriptive names (e.g., SupplementaryFig1.pdf)
- **Organization**: Logical order matching main text references
- **Size Limits**: No specific limit but reasonable file sizes
- **Legends**: Comprehensive legends for all supplementary items

## Submission Process

### Pre-submission Inquiry (Optional)
- **Format**: 1-page summary with 1 key figure
- **Timeline**: Response within 1 week
- **Scope**: Initial assessment of suitability

### Full Submission Requirements
- **Manuscript**: Word document with line numbers
- **Figures**: High-resolution files, correctly named
- **Supplementary Files**: All supporting materials
- **Cover Letter**: Significance statement and competing interests
- **Author Forms**: ICMJE disclosure forms for all authors

### Editorial Process
- **Initial Assessment**: Editorial screening (1-2 weeks)
- **Peer Review**: External review if editorially suitable (3-4 weeks)
- **Decision**: Target 6-8 weeks from submission
- **Revision**: Detailed response to reviewers required

## Acceptance and Publication

### Post-Acceptance Process
- **Production**: Copy-editing and typesetting
- **Proofs**: Author proof corrections
- **Publication**: Online-first publication
- **Print**: Monthly print issue compilation

### Open Access Options
- **Author Choice**: Immediate open access available
- **Fees**: Article processing charges apply
- **Repositories**: Self-archiving policies
- **Compliance**: Funder mandates supported

## Special Considerations

### COVID-19 Research
- **Fast Track**: Expedited editorial and review process
- **Preprints**: Encouraged for rapid dissemination
- **Data Sharing**: Enhanced data sharing expectations

### Clinical Trials
- **Phase III Trials**: Particularly welcome
- **Interim Analyses**: Planned analyses acceptable
- **Negative Results**: Important negative results considered

### Translational Research
- **Bench-to-Bedside**: Studies bridging basic and clinical research
- **Biomarkers**: Validation studies for clinical application  
- **Therapeutic Development**: Early-phase therapeutic studies

### Global Health
- **Resource-Limited Settings**: Research from all global regions
- **Health Disparities**: Studies addressing health equity
- **Implementation Research**: Real-world effectiveness studies
